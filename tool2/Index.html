Step 00 — Scope

Set up your assessment

Name the assessment, set the maturity level you're aiming for, and tell the ledger which EU AI Act obligations apply to your organisation. This determines which controls appear in the EU AI Act section.

Unsure which apply? Leave boxes unchecked and revisit after your AI system inventory (control EU‑1) is complete — reclassify as needed. The prohibited‑practices screen and AI‑literacy controls apply to every organisation regardless of selection.

Step 01 — ISO/IEC 42001:2023

AI Management System controls

Rate current maturity against each clause/Annex A control, 0 (not started) to 4 (managed & optimised). Ratings drive the dashboard and roadmap automatically.

Step 02 — EU Artificial Intelligence Act

Legal obligation controls

Shows controls relevant to the applicability flags you set in Setup. Update the flags there if your AI system inventory changes.
